A small toolkit. A whole world.

3D navigation: Right-drag or Space-drag to move; middle-drag or Alt-left-drag to orbit; scroll to zoom toward your pointer. Hand (H) makes left-drag move the camera. Home resets the view; F focuses the selection; arrow keys move; Q/E rotate. Release a navigation drag near an edge to continue into the next map area. Changing areas saves your draft and starts a fresh undo history. The overview supports click-to-jump. Roofs and scenery can be hidden for interior work. NPCs are shown as markers; textured model faces use simplified colours.

Show slopes keeps hills and valleys shaded while painting paths or placing scenery and NPCs. Turn it off above the map to see plain surface colours. Ground height is shown beside your cursor and at the top of the right panel with every tool. Each menu remembers your chosen asset, rotation and search while this editor is open.

  1. Create area: choose a surface and starting height, then drag over blank map space. Existing land is preserved. Each rectangle is one undo. Match nearby land height helps join new land to an existing coast.
  2. Paint grass, water, paths or floors. Set the brush size on the right. Choose Soft under Brush edge to blend grass, sand and earth tones, while textures use connected tile edges and native diagonal corners where supported. RSC maps do not store transparent texture blends. Adjust Blend strength and repeat strokes for richer colour. Pick colour samples existing terrain; the colour control in Paint selects the nearest RSC ground colour.
  3. Height raises, lowers, smooths or flattens the ground. Hold the brush to keep sculpting; the whole stroke is one undo. Raise and Lower switch out of Hand mode. Adjust brush size and strength on the right. Pick height copies a level from the map for flattening.
  4. Build a house by dragging its footprint. Choose walls, roof and entrance. Standard fence, Low fence and Railings are in Walls; use Open courtyard for the roof when building an enclosure. Automatic adds an entrance only on the ground floor; None makes a fully enclosed room.
  5. Stamp a ready-made house, shop, bridge, dock or tower.
  6. Scenery / People adds the selected asset. NPC cards show Attackable or Non-combat, combat level and ID. Scenery search for fence also finds railings and hedge fences. Scenery thumbnails use the actual RSC models. The model preview and map orientation marker update as you rotate.
  7. NPCs / Items in the map toolbar show or hide placement images in both views. NPC artwork uses each character’s clothing colours; items use their game icons.
  8. People sets an NPC’s wander radius. Use the repair button for existing stationary men and farm animals, or Inspect an individual NPC to change its radius. Save & Test applies roaming to the game.
  9. Select & Inspect a rectangle, then Copy or Save as prefab. Stamps include tiles and placements. Rotate with R.
  10. Select & Inspect a tile to change its elevation or remove an individual placement.
  11. Save draft keeps your work separate. Save & Test checks it, backs up the live map and restarts the game. Log out first.

Ctrl Z / Ctrl Y: undo / redo · Ctrl S: save · Ctrl C / Ctrl V: copy / paste selection · R: rotate · Escape: clear selection · Space-drag or right-drag: pan.

Use a 3D preview to inspect your selected tile or the map centre. Upper floors can be edited with the floor menu. Doors are open entrances; quests and scripted travel still need separate work.

To include scenery, people, doors and items, select SceneryLocs.json, NpcLocs.json, BoundaryLocs.json and GroundItems.json as well.

Imported maps get a separate draft. Export your edited map to use it in your chosen game.
